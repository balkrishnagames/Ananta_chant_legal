Account & Data Deletion

Ananta Chant

You can delete your Ananta Chant account and its associated data at any time, either from inside the app or by emailing us. Both methods remove the same data, described below.

Option 1: Delete In-App (Guest or Google account)

  1. Open Ananta Chant and go to the menu → Settings.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete my account.
  3. Confirm on the dialog that appears. If you signed in with Google some time ago, you may be asked to sign in again first, as a security check before a deletion this permanent.

Your account and its data are deleted immediately once you confirm — this cannot be undone.

Option 2: Request Deletion By Email

If you can't access the app (e.g. you no longer have the device), email balkrishnagames@gmail.com from the address or Google account associated with your Ananta Chant account, with the subject "Delete my account". We'll delete it and confirm by reply, typically within a few business days.
